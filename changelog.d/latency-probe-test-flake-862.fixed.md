- **The latency-probe test of the PR proof harness no longer fails under host load (#862; tooling test only, no change to the Lakehouse binaries or to runtime behaviour).** `test_probe_times_the_proxied_leg_against_the_direct_leg` timed a real 30 ms sleep against the probe's 10 ms tolerance and failed about 5 % of runs on a loaded host (10 of 200, measured), which turned the `api` job's `Test the harness` step into "did not complete" on a good PR. The probe is now checked against a virtual clock that only the fake servers move (exact added time, delay on the proxied leg only, no-delay and swapped-leg controls), plus a one-sided real-clock test that a loaded host cannot break.
